## Deviations from the plan

- One stored quantity, the 95th latency percentile of the full panel called in series, does not reproduce (gap 0.002 s). The plan said that work stops in that case. Work continued, and the paper reports the gap. The cause is not established.
- The plan left the headline suite open and listed the contrasts on all 36 benchmarks; the settings file, frozen before the first run, set the headline suite to 35 benchmarks and added a label budget of 48 groups to the plan's 4, 12 and all.
- Not run: the plan's further appendix rows (pools weighted by confidence, a median pool, the best pair, a vote by organisation) and intervals for contrasts on the benchmarks' own metric. The track on the open subset was run later, first on 2 models and, after the release decision, on 17 (see the table above). On 23 November 2026 two of these were run in `results/followup/`: a pool with equal weight per organisation (a linear pool, not a vote) and intervals for contrasts on the benchmarks' own metric.
- LLMRouterBench's PerfGain and CostSave choose a policy's best setting on the test folds. We report them as that paper defines them, label them so, and keep every other comparison tuned on the validation fold.
